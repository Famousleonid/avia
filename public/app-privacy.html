Privacy Policy — Aviatechnik mobile app

Effective date: July 31, 2026

The Aviatechnik Android application ("the App") is an internal business tool for employees of Aviatechnik Corporation. It is a mobile client for the company's work-order management system and is not intended for the general public.

Data the App collects and why

  • Account credentials. Your work email address and password are used solely to sign in to the company system. After sign-in the App stores an access token in encrypted storage on the device.
  • Work data. Work orders, tasks, part records, serial numbers, and related production data that you view or enter belong to Aviatechnik Corporation and are transmitted to and stored on the company's servers.
  • Photos. The App uses the camera only when you tap a camera button, to document parts and work orders. Photos are uploaded to the company's servers and are not shared with third parties.
  • Push notification token. A Firebase Cloud Messaging token is registered so the company system can deliver work notifications to your device. Delivery is performed by Google's FCM service.

What the App does NOT do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No collection of contacts, location, call logs, or files outside the App.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.

Data storage and security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS) and stored on servers controlled by Aviatechnik Corporation. Access tokens on the device are stored in encrypted storage. Signing out deletes the device session and its push token on the server.

Data retention and deletion

Work data is retained according to the company's record-keeping requirements for aviation maintenance documentation. To delete your account or session data, contact the company administrator; signing out of the App removes the session from your device immediately.

Children

The App is a workplace tool for adult employees and is not directed at children.
